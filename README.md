# Haiku Tower Defense

A single-file tower defense game. Open `haiku-5-5-tower.html` in any modern browser. There is no build step and no dependencies.

**Play it in your browser:** https://artgreen.github.io/haiku-tower-defense/

## How to play

- Pick a tower from the Arsenal panel (keys 1-4), then click open grass to place it. Esc or right-click cancels.
- Click a placed tower to select it, then upgrade (U), move (M) or sell (Delete) it.
- Send the next wave with N. Space pauses, and F toggles 2x speed.
- Enemies avoid the lane with more firepower at the fork. Cover both lanes, or the shared exit road.
- Pressure rises when you clear a wave cleanly while holding lots of unspent gold.
- Survive 20 waves. Bosses appear every 5 waves.
